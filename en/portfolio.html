FINANCE & P&L

What does subordinating e-commerce operations to P&L and contribution margin mean?

Many digital brands celebrate high platform ROAS while their net cash position deteriorates. Subordinating operations to the P&L means evaluating every channel by actual net profit remaining after deducting cost of goods sold (COGS), payment gateway fees, taxes, and shipping expenses. We operate on POAS (Profit on Ad Spend) principles to guarantee that capital allocation builds durable contribution margin.

GENERATIVE AI ENGINES

How does optimization for AI-driven discovery (GEO and AEO) function?

Generative engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews do not process content like standard web indexers: they extract entities, authoritative consensus, and conceptual relationships. We structure e-commerce architecture with advanced Schema.org JSON-LD graph models and deep first-party technical content (E-E-A-T). Consequently, when prospective buyers seek authoritative recommendations, the brand is cited as a primary source.

ENGAGEMENT ARCHITECTURE

What is the most effective engagement structure for our business stage?

Engagements are structured across four operational pillars: Executive Leadership (Fractional or Interim Head of E-commerce / Growth / CRO), Ongoing Strategic Advisory via Squammata for margin governance and commercial pipeline, or Focused Technical Sprints (3 to 4 week deep-dive audits for SEO, Core Web Vitals, CRO, and platform architecture).
